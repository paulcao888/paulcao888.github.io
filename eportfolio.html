01 / ABOUT ME

A practical engineer.
A curious builder.

Kia ora — I’m Alexander (Ganping Cao), based in Auckland, New Zealand. I’m a senior Maintenance Engineer with hands-on experience in semiconductor manufacturing at STMicroelectronics, and I’ve also led a brake manufacturing line. That background taught me to care about uptime, process discipline, and solving problems before they become downtime.

I’m now a postgraduate student in Auckland, deepening my grounding for maintenance and reliability engineering roles here in New Zealand. Alongside study, I build AI-assisted tools and run a one-person company (OPC) that uses AI agents to ship useful software — the same projects featured on my main portfolio.

This ePortfolio gathers who I am, where I’ve worked, what I’m learning, and the work I’m proud to share.
